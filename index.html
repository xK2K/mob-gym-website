01 — Who We Are

More Than A Gym. A Mob.

THE MOB GYM is a full-contact combat sports academy in the heart of Umm Salal Ali. Built for fighters, hobbyists, and everyone in between — we bring world-class coaching across six disciplines under one roof.

From the mats to the cage, every session is designed to push your limits — for kids and adults, beginners and pros.

02 — Disciplines

Sports We Offer

01

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

A ground-based grappling art focused on control, leverage and submissions. BJJ teaches a smaller fighter to overcome a larger opponent using technique — chokes, joint locks and positional dominance instead of strikes.

Grappling · Ground
02

Muay Thai

“The Art of Eight Limbs.” This Thai striking art uses the entire body as a weapon — fists, elbows, knees and shins — combining powerful strikes with clinch work to dominate at every range.

Fists · Elbows · Knees · Shins
03

Kickboxing

A fast, explosive striking sport blending boxing punches with powerful kicks. Builds cardio, coordination and knockout power — a perfect mix of stand-up fighting and high-intensity conditioning.

Punches · Kicks
04

Boxing

The sweet science. Pure hand-striking built on footwork, head movement and timing. Sharpen your jab, cross, hooks and defense while building elite cardiovascular fitness.

Hands · Footwork
05

Fitness

Strength and conditioning programs designed around fighters' needs — functional training, core power, mobility and endurance to keep you strong inside and outside the cage.

Strength · Conditioning
06

Oriental Dance & Zumba

High-energy dance fitness that burns calories while you move to the beat. From expressive oriental dance to electrifying Zumba — fun, rhythmic workouts for everybody and every body.

Cardio · Rhythm
